Starkson Principles

Built on craftsmanship, driven by people.

Starkson Packaging Inc. supports QSR and FMCG customers across Asia with end-to-end packaging expertise, from concept design and consumer testing to quality engineering and just-in-time manufacturing.

PILLAR // 01

Safety & Integrity

We believe every shift must begin and end safely. Our plant adheres to the highest manufacturing and labor standards.

PILLAR // 02

Manufacturing Craft

Our Silang plant manufactures customized boxes, bags, wraps, and containers across paper, paperboard, injection, thermoforming, and flexible converting operations.

PILLAR // 03

Internal Mobility

We prioritize hiring and promoting from within. Our referral programs and skills training ensure paths for advancement.

PILLAR // 04

Built for Growth

Established on July 17, 2013, the company operates under the leadership of Roberto T. Co and continues to grow its manufacturing workforce.

Helpdesk & FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to common questions regarding forms, referrals, and HR support.

Any current regular Starkson employee can refer a candidate using our online Referral Portal. After submission, the HR recruitment team reviews the candidate within 5 business days. When your referral passes screening and completes their 90-day regularization milestone, you receive the corporate referral incentive.
Department supervisors, plant managers, and department heads may log new headcount requests through the MRF Monitor. Scanned hard-copy request slips can be uploaded directly to the tracker for review by People Operations and General Management.
The 201 Files directory contains confidential employee records and personnel files. Access is restricted exclusively to authorized HR Staff and Personnel with designated corporate credentials.
You can visit the Support Desk page, email us directly at starksonpackaginginc@gmail.com, or visit the HR office in person Monday through Friday between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
